## The entry

> CAUTION: Contents believes it is an excellent driver, a talented singer, and a gifted dancer, and is wrong about at least two of these.

135 characters, one line. A deadpan product-label warning about the one
defect every human ships with: quiet overconfidence. The laugh lands in
the last few words — "wrong about at least two of these" — after the
label has solemnly listed three talents nobody asked it to certify.

## How it works

`main.py` is stdlib-only. It validates the line (single line, 1–160
characters, not only white space) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
